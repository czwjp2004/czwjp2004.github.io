About Me

I am an undergraduate student in the Dual-Degree program between Duke Kunshan University and Duke University, pursuing a B.S. in Applied Mathematics and Computational Sciences with a focus on Computer Science. My interests lie at the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Planning, exploring how AI systems can better understand, reason, and make decisions in complex environments.

Recently, I completed an internship at Xiaomi's L3 Autonomous Driving team, where I worked on planning algorithms and contributed to a POMDP-based framework for navigation under uncertainty. I also work as a remote research assistant at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), focusing on multimodal large models and instructional video understanding. Additionally, I am actively involved in the FrontierCS project, contributing to the development of evolving AI benchmarks.
