About Me

I am an undergraduate student in the Dual-Degree program between Duke Kunshan University and Duke University, pursuing a B.S. in Applied Mathematics and Computational Sciences with a focus on Computer Science. My research focuses on LLM Agents, Post-training, and Agentic Reinforcement Learning, with interests in agent skills and memory, efficient reasoning, and long-horizon problem solving.

I am a post-training intern at Meituan and a research assistant at Peking University. My earlier work includes multimodal models and video understanding at SIAT, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and motion planning at Xiaomi.
