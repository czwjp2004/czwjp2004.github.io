About Me

I am an undergraduate student in the Dual-Degree program between Duke Kunshan University and Duke University, pursuing a B.S. in Applied Mathematics and Computational Sciences with a focus on Computer Science. My research interests lie at the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Planning.

Recently, I completed an internship at Xiaomi's L3 Autonomous Driving team, where I worked on motion planning algorithms. I also work as a remote research assistant at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), focusing on multimodal large models and video understanding. Additionally, I am actively involved in the FrontierCS project, contributing to benchmark development for evolving AI systems.
